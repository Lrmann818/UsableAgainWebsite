Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Usable Again is designed to help users break home reset tasks into small, manageable card-based steps.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use Usable Again.

Overview

Usable Again does not require an account, login, or profile to use the app.

We do not collect personal information through the app itself. The app is designed to work locally on your device.

Information we collect

Usable Again does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or files.

If you contact us for support or feedback by email, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message content, device details, app version, or any screenshots you attach. We use that information only to respond to your request and improve the app.

Information we do not collect

Usable Again does not use:

  • User accounts
  • Third-party advertising
  • Third-party tracking
  • Location services
  • Contact access
  • Photo library access
  • Microphone access
  • Camera access
  • User-generated public content
  • External payment links

In-app purchases

Usable Again offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase called the Premium Pack.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Apple may process purchase-related information according to Apple's own privacy policies and App Store terms.

Local app data

The app stores a few preferences locally on your device, such as your selected theme and app icon.

This information is used to keep the app working as expected and is not sent to us through the app. Device backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include these preferences.

Premium Pack access is checked through Apple's StoreKit. Apple keeps the record of your purchase.

Third-party services

Usable Again uses Apple platform services, including StoreKit, to support App Store purchases and purchase restoration.

If future versions of the app add services such as accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, or feedback forms, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are used.

This website

This website does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, and its fonts are served from this site rather than a third-party font service.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the site, GitHub and Cloudflare process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, and Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies. See GitHub's Privacy Statement and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Usable Again is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Do Not Track

Usable Again and this website do not track you over time or across third-party websites or apps, so browser Do Not Track signals do not change how they work.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests to correct or delete information you have sent us, support, or feedback, contact:
